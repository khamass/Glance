# Glance launch kit

## Short announcement

**Meet Glance — a little eye contact, a little personality.**

Zoom in and let your character meet the camera with their head and eyes. Pick a personality mood, add occasional expressions, or build your own selection of unlocked emotes. Optional close-up camera controls include smooth transitions and fine zoom steps.

Free and open source, by **Macki**. Glance v1.0.0 is available from the custom repository. Configure with `/glance`.

Project and installation: https://github.com/khamass/Glance

Use this announcement only where community rules permit plugin promotion. Attach real in-game footage; this document is not evidence of a recorded demo.

## Directory listing copy

Name: Glance

Author: Macki

Category: Camera / Character / Expressions

Summary: Camera-facing gaze, personality moods and customizable close-up framing for idle character moments.

Description: Glance activates head and eye gaze when you zoom in and stand still. Choose from eighteen mood menu options, including Custom and gaze-only mode, or search the installed game's unlocked performance emotes. Configure expression frequency and timing, opt into body animations, and use optional extended close-up distance, camera-angle controls and fine zoom with smooth transitions. Existing game activity exclusions pause the relevant features. It is distributed through a custom repository and is not an official Dalamud listing.

Project/source: https://github.com/khamass/Glance

Latest release: https://github.com/khamass/Glance/releases/latest

Installer manifest: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/khamass/Glance/main/repo.json

License: MIT — https://github.com/khamass/Glance/blob/main/LICENSE

Icon: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/khamass/Glance/main/images/icon.png

Support: https://github.com/khamass/Glance/issues/new/choose

Dependencies: XIVLauncher/Dalamud; targets Dalamud API 15. No other plugin dependency.

Limitations: Body motions can turn/move the character, need targets or loop; facial cleanup does not stop every body motion. Other camera plugins may interfere. A game crash during close-up can leave the temporary camera-angle setting in place. Extended camera bounds do not meet official Dalamud repository restrictions. Do not claim guaranteed safety or compatibility with every patch.

Disclosure: Source developed with substantial AI assistance and human design direction/in-game feedback. Icon generated with AI assistance.

## Twenty-second demo storyboard

Record real gameplay with other camera plugins' angle/zoom controls disabled for the demonstration. Use a normal idle location, a clear background and a non-explicit character outfit. Hide identifying chat if desired.

1. 0–4 seconds: start at ordinary third-person distance. Caption: “A little eye contact.”
2. 4–9 seconds: slowly zoom into close-up. Show the smooth transition and the character meeting the camera.
3. 9–13 seconds: rotate slightly to either side to demonstrate gaze tracking.
4. 13–17 seconds: show an actual triggered wink or a chosen expression. Caption: “A little personality.” Wait for the real trigger rather than presenting an unrelated emote as automatic behavior.
5. 17–20 seconds: zoom back out. End card: “Glance by Macki · Free & open source · github.com/khamass/Glance”.

Suggested export: MP4, 1080p, 30 or 60 fps, 15–25 seconds. Keep a clean full-resolution source. Add the finished video near the top of README only after it exists and has been checked. No placeholder demo link is published.
